MacCrab v1.21.6-rc.15

This candidate supersedes the unpublished rc.14 after its installed-host
cold-restart audit found one release-blocking finalized-store reopen defect.

- A durable `schema_finalized = 1` event store now takes a validation-only
  reopen path before transition-specific checkpoint and scratch-space gates.
  The complete journal schema, rollback barrier and DML guards are still
  verified fail-closed before that fast path is accepted.
- A dashboard or other read-only client may pin healthy retained WAL frames
  without forcing the Endpoint Security extension into a `storage_not_ready`
  restart loop when no schema transition work remains.
- Regressions cover a genuinely pinned-WAL finalized reopen and rejection of a
  forged/incomplete finalized schema. The exact installed rc.14 event family
  was also replayed from a disposable copy and passed its full authenticated
  journal and FTS pre-producer validation under the measured 353-MiB ceiling.
- rc.14's retained-reserve rounding and exact-candidate containment corrections
  remain included.

All rc.13 evidence-integrity, bounded-memory, storage-recovery, read-only
tooling, credential-sanitization and release-qualification changes remain
included.

Minimum macOS 13.0. This release candidate must pass clean CI, signing,
notarization, exact-candidate containment, cold restart and the uninterrupted
installed-host qualification before publication.
