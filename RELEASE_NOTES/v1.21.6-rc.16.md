MacCrab v1.21.6-rc.16

This candidate supersedes the unpublished rc.15 after its installed-host cold
restart found a separate reader-pinned WAL failure in startup retention.

- Routine event-journal expiry now proves a drained WAL before beginning a
  rollup/delete transaction. A dashboard or other read-only client pinning the
  prior WAL snapshot causes that sweep to defer with zero mutations instead of
  terminating the Endpoint Security extension.
- Deferred expiry remains bounded and convergent: the physical family cap is
  still checked fail-closed, no additional WAL-producing transaction starts
  behind the pin, and the next sweep resumes after the reader releases it.
- The installed failure shape is now covered end to end: finalized-journal
  reopen, pre-producer recovery, pinned-WAL startup expiry with unchanged
  evidence, reader release, and successful subsequent expiry.
- rc.15's finalized-schema validation-only reopen correction remains included.

Minimum macOS 13.0. This release candidate must pass clean CI, signing,
notarization, exact-candidate containment, cold restart and the uninterrupted
installed-host qualification before publication.
