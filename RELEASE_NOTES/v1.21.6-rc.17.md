MacCrab v1.21.6-rc.17

This candidate supersedes the unpublished rc.16 after installed-host
qualification exposed two defects in the qualification recorder itself.

- Durable pending sequence steps are now distinguished from transient writer
  backlog. Qualification permits this legitimate detection working set while
  still proving exact conservation, matching pending-depth telemetry, zero
  in-flight work, zero shed/eviction, and nominal sequence continuity.
- macOS process-resource sampling now binds the complete 296-byte
  `rusage_info_v4` structure. The previous 256-byte buffer allowed
  `proc_pid_rusage` to overwrite 40 bytes of Python heap, which could crash the
  recorder during interpreter shutdown after reporting a failure.
- The rc.16 reader-pinned WAL startup-expiry correction remains included and
  passed its installed cold-start audit against retained production data.

Minimum macOS 13.0. This release candidate must pass clean CI, signing,
notarization, exact-candidate containment, cold restart and the uninterrupted
installed-host qualification before publication.
